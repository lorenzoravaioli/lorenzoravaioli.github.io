About Me

I'm a 23-year-old aerospace engineering student at the University of Bologna. My main focus is everything that flies: designing and building the electronics that make rockets work.

My work centers on avionics, embedded systems, and PCB design — from high-density flight computers to telemetry and recovery systems that survive real launch conditions.
